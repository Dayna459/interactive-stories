The Ugly Duckling

A story about belonging and discovering who you are.

A Little Duckling

Once upon a time, on a peaceful farm beside a sparkling pond, a mother duck waited patiently for her eggs to hatch. One by one, the little ducklings appeared. But one egg was much larger than the others.

The Different One

At last, the large egg cracked open. Out came a duckling who looked very different from his brothers and sisters. He was bigger, greyer and rather clumsy. The other animals stared at him.

Feeling Alone

The little duckling tried his best to fit in, but everywhere he went he felt unwelcome. The other ducks laughed at him and called him ugly. He began to wonder if there was somewhere he truly belonged.

A New Journey

One morning, the duckling decided to leave. He travelled through fields and forests, searching for a place where he could feel at home. The journey was difficult, but he kept going.

A Cold Winter

Winter arrived, bringing icy winds and snow. The little duckling was alone and frightened. He found a quiet place beside a frozen pond and dreamed of warmer days.

You Belong

When spring arrived, the duckling discovered something wonderful. He had grown into a beautiful young swan. The swans welcomed him into their family, and for the first time, he felt completely at home.
